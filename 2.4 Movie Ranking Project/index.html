Best Movies According to Me

My top 3 movies of all-time

Spirited Away

A movie by my favourite anime director Hayao Miyazaki, the movie aabout emotions. imagination and connection Something which link our soul to such movies. Worth watching it.

Jurassic Park(1993)

A cool sci-fi movie which make us imagine the scenario of what if this happen? One who keeps interest in Dinosaurs and gaining knowledge about them is worth to give a watch to it. If this spark your interest, you can watch other parts too 😊

Jumanji(1995)

A fantacy adventure movie, all about a magical, jungle-themed game that brings wild and dangerous animals, plants, and chaos into the real world with every turn. Players must finish the game to stop the destruction and reverse the effects. Give this one a try and you would love it.
